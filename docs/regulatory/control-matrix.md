## Mandatory per-rule evidence (before activation)
Official URL/document identifier; verified version and effective dates; section/article; applicability and exceptions; record series; technical constraint; approval workflow; Bank owner; test ID; decision evidence; reviewer; release gate; effective-dated version and rollback/impact analysis. No universal 10-year retention period. An open or unverified law is never an executable JRA configuration.
